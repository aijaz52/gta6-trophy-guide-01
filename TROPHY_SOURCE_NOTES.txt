This is a guide to all trophies and achievements in [Grand Theft Auto VI](https://gta.guide/gta-6) on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with unlock conditions and tips.

## Key facts
* A total of 55 trophies and achievements; on PS5 this is 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, and 46 bronze trophies
* Xbox Series X|S achievements add up to 1,000G, and the equivalent of the platinum trophy is the Mythic Achievement
* There are no GTA Online trophies: [GTA Online](https://gta.guide/gta-online) will launch later, so the list will probably grow
* About 28 rewards are hidden, mostly tied to the story
* To get platinum, you need to obtain all other trophies, including 100% completion
* Cheats disable trophies and saving, so it's best to keep them off while you hunt for trophies

## Things to know
* According to GTABase, you can only play the story once: the game has no difficulty levels, no New Game+ and no mission replays, though you can keep exploring in free roam after the credits
* Positive Mental Attitude may be the only missable trophy: in Chapter 1, accept the invitation to meditate with [Lucia](https://gta.guide/gta-6/characters/lucia-caminos)
* Chapter, mission and character names give away the story, so the list below contains spoilers

## Platinum trophy
### Vice Got a Price!
* Type: Platinum (PS5), Mythic Achievement (Xbox)
* Condition: Obtain all other trophies and achievements
* How to get: Most of the time will go into 100% completion; by GTABase's estimate, the whole list takes about 200 hours
* Official description: Congratulations! You're a Leonida Legend

## Progress and activities
### A Long Road
* Type: Gold · 100G
* Condition: Reach 100% completion
* How to get: The checklist appears after the story in Progress > Stats > 100% Completion; everything done earlier already counts, and nothing is missable. You have to complete all robberies (8 bank vaults and 39 gang operations), find the underwater discoveries, earn gold in every shooting range challenge, finish all drone races and do Bret's 15 hunting tasks.
Other categories need only part of the total: for example, 70 of 80 challenges and 15 of 18 meditations

### Cunning Stunts
* Type: Silver · 40G
* Condition: Complete 250 stunts
* How to get: The mix doesn't matter, as long as the total reaches 250 out of 397: jumps, flights under bridges, knife-edge flights and base jumps all count. Flights under bridges are the quickest way to build up the total. The 100% checklist needs 300 stunts, so it's worth going past 250

### Master of None
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Do each kind of activity at least once
* How to get: Nine activity types count: fishing, hunting, golf, shooting range, pool, basketball (one shot), an arcade game, a race win and a stunt

### The Shootist
* Type: Silver · 40G
* Condition: Get gold in every shooting range challenge
* How to get: You need 42 gold medals across 12 shooting ranges; ranges also raise your Focus level

### Top Fun
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Win a Top Fun drone race in each of the locations
* How to get: There are 14 locations and one win at each is enough; the 100% checklist needs all 42 races

### Earn Cash, Not Kudos
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Win 20 different races
* How to get: There are 40 races to choose from; winning the same race again doesn't count

### Baited My Tackle
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Win a fishing challenge against Cal
* How to get: After you meet Cal in the Chapter 1 mission Feng Shui, he texts you a fishing spot; play through his fishing quests and beat him in the challenge

### Stick Fiddler
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Complete an arcade game
* How to get: Any of the four will do: Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III and Time Jackers Rewind on the TV at home, or Bang For Your Buck on arcade cabinets around the state

## Character and stranger missions
### Road to Rehabilitation
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Complete the final mission for Andy
* How to get: Lucia-only quests that start in Chapter 3 with a text from Andy. There are five: Climbing the Cellerichain, Pay the Cellerica Fee, Peach!, Read the Label and Puff Puff Pass; the last one gives the trophy

### This Was No Boat Accident
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Complete the final mission for Kenton
* How to get: In Chapter 1 Kenton asks you to recover treasure from 7 shipwrecks: dive to the purple markers and dig it up with a metal detector. He texts again later, so you can finish the job then

### The Lone Rider
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Complete the final mission for Billy
* How to get: Five jobs start at the Thrillbilly Mudclub after the Coffee Break mission in Chapter 2; the fifth one, Hometown Heroes, gives the trophy

### Strangers & Freaks
* Type: Silver · 30G
* Condition: Complete 8 stranger mission strands across the [State of Leonida](https://gta.guide/gta-6/locations/state-of-leonida)
* How to get: The chains of Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count, for example; those of Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie do not. The full list of qualifying chains is still being confirmed, so follow the counter in the trophy list

### Supply & Demand
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Complete the final mission for Randy
* How to get: Deliver the 16 sports cars on his list to his garage in the west of [Vice City](https://gta.guide/gta-6/locations/vice-city) ; scanning cars with the WAiNK app shows which ones are on the list

### Spin On This!
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Complete the final mission for Tyrone
* How to get: A job only for [Jason Duval](https://gta.guide/gta-6/characters/jason-duval) : after the Here, Kitty Kitty mission in Chapter 3, destroy the synthetic labs around Vice City; his last job, Shut Down the Labs, gives the trophy

### Positive Mental Attitude
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Meditate at every spot across Leonida
* How to get: Bring the counter up to 18; meditating together (Jason and Lucia) counts twice. The trophy may be missable: in Chapter 1, the morning after the Down to Earth mission, accept Lucia's invitation to meditate on the balcony. It's not confirmed whether the trophy requires this meditation, so it's safest not to skip it

### Fair Game
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Hunt a unique animal for Bret
* How to get: Bret's hunting jobs start after the Coffee Break mission in Chapter 2; it's not yet clear which animal the trophy needs, but the 100% checklist needs all 15 jobs anyway

### Don't Touch My Deck
* Type: Silver · 30G
* Condition: Collect 3 sets of collectible cards
* How to get: Any three sets will do; progress is visible in the Collections section of the Progress menu

## Challenges, partner and collections
### You WAiNKer!
* Type: Silver · 30G
* Condition: Use WAiNK It in 100 locations
* How to get: Any 100 locations count, whether landmarks, oddities, sculptures or No Flusher graffiti

### One Last Thing
* Type: Bronze · 5G
* Condition: Complete 50 challenges
* How to get: Out of 80 challenges; many are completed naturally as you play, but for 100% you need 70

### Hot Together
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Escape a 4-star wanted level together with your partner
* How to get: Available after the Coffee Break mission in Chapter 2: call your partner, get 4 stars and escape. It only counts once you leave the red zone. A bank vault robbery always gives 4 stars, so the trophy often unlocks there

### Retail Therapy
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Rob 3 stores in a row together with your partner without losing your wanted level
* How to get: The three jewelry stores in the north of Vice City work well; the trophy may pop up with a delay of about 30 seconds

### Whip It Good
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Scan 100 different vehicles with the WAiNK app
* How to get: You don't need to steal cars; just stand by a busy road. For 100% you need 200 models

### Gunslinger
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Kill 5 enemies during one activation of Combat Focus
* How to get: The effect doesn't last long, so fill the Focus meter first and grab an assault rifle; gang members from gang operations are easy targets

## Robberies and exploration
### Catch Me If You Can
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Perform an unplanned takeoff from VCIA
* How to get: Steal a large passenger jet parked at a gate's jet bridge, take off and get about 1 km away from the airport

### Challenging Tank
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Kill enemies from a stolen tank
* How to get: The tanks stand on the southern edge of the Mile Zero Air Station base on the southwestern island of Mariana County
* Official description: Kinda hot in these Rhinos

### Sneakthief
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Rob a jewelry store while remaining undetected
* How to get: You need the Snap Gun from Yasmani's missions in Chapter 3: use it on the back door, then find a note with the safe code in the storeroom

### Maximum Withdrawal
* Type: Silver · 40G
* Condition: Rob 5 bank vaults and get away
* How to get: Vaults are unlocked by the optional mission That's Where The Money Is… in Chapter 4; it's convenient to split the loot with a partner. For 100% you need all 8 vaults

### A Classic Blitz Play
* Type: Bronze · 10G
* Condition: Rob an armored truck and get away
* How to get: Robbing a bank vault also counts if you then escape; you can also blow up the truck with a sticky bomb

### The Goodtime State
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Explore all of Leonida
* How to get: Clear the fog from the entire map; the fastest way is to fly a plane over it

### Pest Control
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Rob an illegal operation belonging to each gang
* How to get: You need one operation from each of the 9 gangs; for 100% all 39 are required

### Dime on the Dollar
* Type: Bronze · 20G
* Condition: Earn $250,000 by laundering Marked Bills through contacts

### On the Spot
* Type: Bronze · 15G
* Condition: Register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app
* How to get: The app comes from the Moses mission in Chapter 2: take stolen cars to his garage in Port Gellhorn. You need free garage space, and each registration costs money, so use cheap cars and bikes
