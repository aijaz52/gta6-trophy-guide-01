# GTA VI trophy research notebook

Source context: the supplied guide, "All GTA 6 trophies and achievements," dated November 18, 2026 at 5:40 PM, lists an approximately 12-minute reading time. These notes reorganize that material; uncertain requirements below remain uncertain.

SOURCES AND SCOPE
The supplied guide covers Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, including unlock requirements and suggested methods: https://gta.guide/gta-6
Its totals are 55 rewards: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze on PS5, with 1,000G on Xbox. Xbox calls the platinum counterpart a Mythic Achievement; the source gives it no separate G value. Roughly 28 rewards are hidden, mainly story rewards.
The list excludes GTA Online, which the source says launches later; additional rewards may follow: https://gta.guide/gta-online
GTABase is the attributed source for the roughly 200-hour estimate and the claim that there are no difficulty settings, New Game+ or mission replays. The story is played once, with free exploration available after the credits.

PLANNING NOTES
Platinum requires every other reward, including full completion. Cheats disable both saving and trophies, so leave them off during a trophy run. Names of chapters, missions and characters below reveal story details.
The possible missable is Positive Mental Attitude: accept Lucia's Chapter 1 meditation invitation. The source has not confirmed whether that specific session is required.
The post-story checklist is under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Earlier work carries over, and the source says that checklist has no missable items. It requires all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, underwater discoveries, every shooting-range gold, all 42 drone races and Bret's 15 hunts. Some categories need only a subset: 70 of 80 challenges and 15 of 18 meditations. The stunt target for full completion is 300, and vehicle scanning requires 200 models.

PLATINUM / MYTHIC — 1 REWARD

01. Vice Got a Price! — Platinum on PS5; Mythic Achievement on Xbox.
Collect the other 54 rewards. Full completion accounts for much of the estimated 200 hours attributed to GTABase. The supplied official text reads: "Congratulations! You're a Leonida Legend".

GOLD — 2 REWARDS

02. A Long Road — Gold, 100G.
Reach 100% completion using the checklist and totals above. The checklist becomes visible after the story, but actions taken earlier already contribute.

03. You Gotta Know When to Get Off — Gold, 50G.
Finish the final mission. Free roam remains available after the credits for collecting remaining rewards, subject to the meditation uncertainty noted above.

SILVER — 6 REWARDS

04. Cunning Stunts — Silver, 40G.
Finish 250 of the 397 stunts in any combination of jumps, under-bridge flights, knife-edge flights and base jumps. Under-bridge flights are the suggested quick option. Continue to 300 for the full-completion checklist.

05. The Shootist — Silver, 40G.
Earn gold in every shooting-range challenge: 42 gold medals spread over 12 ranges. Range practice also increases Focus.

06. Maximum Withdrawal — Silver, 40G.
Escape successfully after robbing 5 bank vaults. The optional Chapter 4 mission That's Where The Money Is… opens vault robberies. A partner can share the haul; all 8 vaults are needed for 100%.

07. Don't Touch My Deck — Silver, 30G.
Finish any 3 collectible-card sets. Check Collections in the Progress menu to see how the sets are coming along.

08. You WAiNKer! — Silver, 30G.
Use WAiNK It at 100 locations. Landmarks, oddities, sculptures and No Flusher graffiti all qualify; any combination reaching 100 works.

09. Strangers & Freaks — Silver, 30G.
Complete 8 qualifying stranger mission strands around Leonida. Examples that count are Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete. Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie do not count. The complete qualifying list is still unconfirmed; use the trophy counter to track it.

BRONZE: STORY PROGRESSION — 11 REWARDS

10. Just Like Paradise — Bronze, 5G.
Finish Gone Fishing, one of Chapter 1's early missions. The source describes this unlock as unavoidable.

11. Paradise Lost — Bronze, 20G.
Complete Chapter 1; the unlock occurs after What's in the Bag?, the first mission in Chapter 2.

12. T**s Ass Drug Money — Bronze, 20G.
Complete Chapter 2. The reward arrives at Chapter 3's opening after Welcome to Vice City. Trophy lists mask the name with asterisks.

13. Vice City, Baby! — Bronze, 20G.
Complete Chapter 3. The unlock follows Ocean Beach and Another Life, the first two missions in Chapter 4.

14. Top Of The World, Ma — Bronze, 20G.
Finish Chapter 4.

15. Trust — Bronze, 10G.
Complete Breakfast Run in Chapter 2.

16. Break the Bank — Bronze, 25G.
Complete The Cartel Heist in Chapter 3.

17. Family Matters — Bronze, 15G.
Finish the Chapter 4 mission Child's Play.

18. DWNFALL — Bronze, 15G.
Finish Headliners in Chapter 4.

19. Hi-Jacked — Bronze, 25G.
Complete VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4.

20. Snow Storm — Bronze, 25G.
Complete Operation Savage Glory in Chapter 5.

BRONZE: PEOPLE AND SIDE ACTIVITIES — 12 REWARDS

21. Positive Mental Attitude — Bronze, 10G.
Meditate throughout Leonida until the counter reaches 18. A shared session with Jason and Lucia adds two. In Chapter 1, accept Lucia's balcony invitation on the morning after Down to Earth: whether that session is essential remains unconfirmed, making this a possible missable.

22. Road to Rehabilitation — Bronze, 15G.
Finish Andy's last mission. His Lucia-only chain starts with a Chapter 3 text and runs through Climbing the Cellerichain, Pay the Cellerica Fee, Peach!, Read the Label and Puff Puff Pass. Completing Puff Puff Pass awards the trophy.

23. This Was No Boat Accident — Bronze, 15G.
Finish Kenton's final assignment. In Chapter 1 he requests treasure from 7 wrecks; dive at purple markers and use a metal detector to dig it up. A later text lets you finish the work.

24. The Lone Rider — Bronze, 15G.
Finish Billy's last job. His five-job chain begins at Thrillbilly Mudclub after Coffee Break in Chapter 2 and ends with Hometown Heroes, which gives the reward.

25. Supply & Demand — Bronze, 20G.
Finish Randy's final mission by delivering his 16 listed sports cars to his garage in western Vice City. Scan cars in WAiNK to identify requested models.

26. Spin On This! — Bronze, 15G.
Finish Tyrone's last mission as Jason. After Here, Kitty Kitty in Chapter 3, destroy synthetic labs around Vice City; the concluding job is Shut Down the Labs.

27. Fair Game — Bronze, 15G.
Hunt a unique animal for Bret. His jobs open after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. The particular animal required is still unclear; full completion needs all 15 hunting jobs regardless.

28. Baited My Tackle — Bronze, 10G.
Beat Cal in a fishing challenge. Meet him in Chapter 1's Feng Shui, follow the fishing-spot text and continue his fishing quests until the challenge is available.

29. Master of None — Bronze, 20G.
Try each of nine activity types: fishing, hunting, golf, the shooting range, pool, basketball (a single shot), an arcade game, a race win and a stunt.

30. Top Fun — Bronze, 15G.
Win a Top Fun drone race at every location. One win at each of 14 locations earns this trophy, whereas 100% completion requires all 42 races.

31. Earn Cash, Not Kudos — Bronze, 20G.
Win 20 distinct races from the 40 available. Repeating a victory at the same race adds nothing to this count.

32. Stick Fiddler — Bronze, 10G.
Complete one of four arcade games: Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III or Time Jackers Rewind on the home TV, or Bang For Your Buck at arcade cabinets across the state.

BRONZE: PARTNERS, CHALLENGES AND EXPLORATION — 11 REWARDS

33. Hot Together — Bronze, 20G.
Lose a 4-star wanted level while with your partner. After Coffee Break in Chapter 2, call the partner, reach four stars and escape beyond the red zone. Bank-vault robberies always give four stars and can provide the opportunity.

34. Retail Therapy — Bronze, 20G.
With your partner, rob 3 stores consecutively while keeping the wanted level active. The three jewelry shops in northern Vice City are suggested. The unlock can lag by about 30 seconds.

35. One Last Thing — Bronze, 5G.
Complete 50 of the 80 challenges. Many accrue during ordinary play; the 100% target is 70.

36. Whip It Good — Bronze, 20G.
Scan 100 different vehicles using WAiNK. Stealing them is unnecessary; watching a busy road is enough. Full completion calls for 200 distinct models.

37. Gunslinger — Bronze, 10G.
Defeat 5 enemies within a single Combat Focus activation. Fill the meter before starting, use an assault rifle and consider gang-operation enemies as targets because the effect is brief.

38. Catch Me If You Can — Bronze, 10G.
Make an unplanned departure from VCIA: steal a large passenger jet at a gate's jet bridge, take off and travel roughly 1 km away from the airport.

39. Challenging Tank — Bronze, 10G.
Kill enemies using a stolen tank. Tanks sit along the southern edge of Mile Zero Air Station, on Mariana County's southwestern island. Supplied official text: "Kinda hot in these Rhinos".

40. Sneakthief — Bronze, 10G.
Rob a jewelry shop without detection. Obtain the Snap Gun through Yasmani's Chapter 3 missions, open the back door with it and locate the safe-code note in the storeroom.

41. A Classic Blitz Play — Bronze, 10G.
Rob an armored truck and escape; a sticky bomb can open the truck. The source also says escaping after a bank-vault robbery counts.

42. The Goodtime State — Bronze, 20G.
Explore Leonida by clearing all map fog. Flying over the state is the suggested fastest method.

43. Pest Control — Bronze, 20G.
Rob one illegal operation for each of the 9 gangs. Full completion expands that requirement to all 39 operations.

BRONZE: MONEY, STATS AND UNRESOLVED DETAILS — 12 REWARDS

44. Dime on the Dollar — Bronze, 20G.
Earn a cumulative $250,000 from laundering Marked Bills through contacts. Chapter 3 supplies the Dye Pack Jammer; rob banks and Payday Loans, then sell the bills to fences. Yasmani offers the highest payout.

45. Treat Yo Self — Bronze, 15G.
Spend $100,000 in total across stores. Weapons, vehicle modifications and tattoos are suggested ways to spend quickly.

46. Little Boxes — Bronze, 15G.
Purchase all 8 garages listed in Vice City Real Estate, which opens after Daily Driver in Chapter 2. Budget about $160,000; the four story-provided garages do not count.

47. On the Spot — Bronze, 15G.
Register 10 vehicles through Scooter Bros. A Chapter 2 mission for Moses supplies the app; bring stolen vehicles to his Port Gellhorn garage. Registration needs spare garage space and a fee, so cheap cars and bikes keep costs down.

48. Personal Best — Bronze, 5G.
Maximize Strength for both protagonists. Train at fitness clubs with the partner present; training one increases the other's stat too. Supplied official text: "A partnership with mass appeal".

49. Actions Speak Louder — Bronze, 5G.
Perform 500 actions that affect the Criminal Profile. Normal play is expected to cover this. Avoid reaching Irredeemable because that profile state is permanent. Supplied official text: "Choices made (for better or worse)".

50. In The Beginning… — Bronze, 5G.
Have a cocktail overlooking Ocean Beach. The exact procedure has not been confirmed.

51. Capital Offense — Bronze, 5G.
Drive a showroom car through its display window. Detailed steps remain unconfirmed. Supplied official text: "Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style".

52. Let's Go Bowling — Bronze, 5G.
Rob Kingpin Bowling in Port Gellhorn and evade police. Take the watch, Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun and safe. The source's trophy description instead names Kolt & Associates; retain that naming discrepancy. Supplied official text: "Follow-through at the Kolt & Associates' bowling alley".

53. Bad Luck, I Guess — Bronze, 5G.
The unlock condition is undisclosed and the method is unconfirmed. Supplied official text: "Second time's the charm".

54. The Truth Is Out There — Bronze, 5G.
The unlock condition is undisclosed and the method is unconfirmed. Supplied official text: "What the hell just happened?".

55. Ten Pin — Bronze, 5G.
The description suggests a melee drive-by, but the steps have not been confirmed. Supplied official text: "Bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by".
